# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

First release.

- Radial app switcher for macOS and Windows: hold the shortcut, move to a slice, let go.
- Default shortcut: ⌥ Tab on macOS, Ctrl + Alt + Tab on Windows. Four alternatives in Preferences.
- Eight slices with app icon and name. More than eight apps spread over pages: press the shortcut's key again or scroll.
- Slices show apps (most recently used first) or, optionally, individual windows.
- Pinned favourites take fixed slices and launch the app when it is closed.
- Quick tap jumps back to the previous app. Esc, or letting go over the middle, cancels.
- Five themes shared with DragWheel.
- No network access, no telemetry. macOS Apps mode needs no permission at all.
